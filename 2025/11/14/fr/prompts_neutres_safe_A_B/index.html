🟩 VERSION A — PROMPT 100% NEUTRE (ULTRA SAFE)

A continuous animated sequence in a handcrafted painted style.
Soft colors, gentle atmosphere, smooth movements.
Children of varied origins stand together in a calm outdoor setting.
The camera slowly pans down from the sky toward the group.
The scene transitions gradually to children walking side by side,
with small glowing particles in the air.

Next, a close-up shows two hands joining, with the background fading
into abstract light shapes.

The environment brightens gradually into a warm daytime scene where the children
move in a circular pattern with joy.

Then the viewpoint shifts to a calmer moment where they support each other
with friendly gestures.

The environment darkens slowly into a night scene with many small lights above,
and a child looking upward.

and the children run happily through an open field.

no sharp cuts, no references to specific stories or styles.

🟨 VERSION B — PROMPT POÉTIQUE + NEUTRE (SAFE & BEAUTIFUL)

A continuous animation painted in gentle hand-crafted watercolor textures,
with soft outlines and calm atmospheric lighting.

The film begins with a vast, deep sky slowly revealed as the camera slides downward,
introducing a group of children standing together in quiet unity.
Warm and cool tones blend fluidly, creating a peaceful mood.

The scene transitions smoothly as the group begins walking through an open landscape
while delicate glowing particles drift around them.
Brush-like textures flow with the movement, keeping everything soft and dreamlike.

A gentle zoom brings two hands into focus, meeting with tenderness.
Behind them, abstract light forms drift in slow motion,
like pigments dissolving in water.

Colors shift toward warm morning tones as the children gather in a circle,
moving with joy on a sunlit plain.
Light spreads across their faces in soft gradients of gold and pastel.

The atmosphere becomes more intimate as the children share gentle gestures
of care and kindness, surrounded by warm diffuse radiance.

Then the world gradually fades into deep nighttime hues.
A child gazes upward as countless small lights appear overhead,
glowing like hand-painted specks of ink on textured paper.
The moment feels calm and contemplative.

The final transition brings warm daylight again,
as the children run freely across an open field filled with brightness.
The camera rises gently, letting the scene dissolve into warm luminous tones.

All transitions rely on slow color blending, watercolor diffusion,
and smooth camera motion — no sharp edits, no direct references,
fully neutral and original.
